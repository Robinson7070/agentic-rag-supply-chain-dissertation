NOTES FOR DISSERTATION METHODOLOGY SECTION
================================================================================

1. Documents were generated programmatically using Python (ReportLab) to
   ensure systematic variation and full reproducibility.

2. Five distinct visual/structural formats were used across suppliers to
   test document ingestion robustness under realistic format variation —
   mirroring how real-world suppliers each use their own invoice templates,
   rather than assuming a single standardised format.

3. Ground truth labels were assigned at creation time, enabling precise,
   objective evaluation of the agentic RAG system's anomaly detection
   accuracy, false positive rate, and retrieval accuracy against a
   known-correct answer key.

4. The dataset deliberately balances clean baseline cases (Category 1)
   against single-error cases (Categories 2-5) and compound multi-error
   cases (Category 6), allowing evaluation of whether system performance
   degrades as task complexity increases — directly addressing the
   research question on agentic vs. standard RAG performance differences.
